Restricting the primary Negative-Binomial model to the post-ramp window (2025 Q1 - 2026 Q2, n = 6) gives a slope of **+2.9%** per quarter (95% CI -3.8% to +10.2%, p = 0.402), versus **-10.7%** per quarter (p = 0.007) over the full study period. The apparent decline over the full study period appears to be driven substantially by the elevated rates observed during the 2024 launch period: once those two quarters are excluded, the remaining six quarters show a much weaker, no-longer-significant trend. Rates from 2025 onward appear comparatively stable. This is consistent with a launch/ramp-period effect rather than a continuing decline, though it does not establish causation.
